# ui

HUD, menus and overlays in HTML and CSS, on top of the canvas. The look follows [art direction](../../docs/art-direction.md).

- `hud.ts`, `hud.css`: builds the HUD and keeps it in step with pointer lock, the benchmark, the camera and the graphics preset.
- `player-hud/`: the in-game HUD of the reference (vitals, compass, objective, hotbar) as a pure view. `placeholder-hud.ts` drives it with the game's real heading, the player's own Health and Energy, the crew's respawn charges and the screen of a player who is down, the player's building hands (the hotbar selection, the crosshair, Build Mode and the caption), the pulse blaster's sight and hit marker, the crew's pool as the counts on the first four hotbar slots (a padlock instead of a count on a material that is not researched yet), the crew's research as a pill under the respawn charges, the card under the crosshair that says what E would strip, and made-up numbers for what does not exist yet (the hotbar's tools after the blaster).
- `vitals-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the player's meters, as plain data in and out: the Health segments and the Energy bar, the tone each turns when it runs low, and the "Charging", "Low energy", "Out of energy" and "Shaken" icons.
- `run-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the run, as plain data in and out: the day and night pill by the compass, the Outpost Core's gauge and the crew's respawn pips under it, the screen of a player who is down (`downForHud`), the objective in the banner, the line on the pause card, the results of a lost run (`resultsForHud`, now with the materials gathered and the research earned) and the message when the light changes, a wave is coming, a player goes down or comes back, the run ends, or the player's own strip of a heap of debris goes into the pool (`harvestMessage`).
- `build-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the building hands, as plain data in and out so Vitest can run it: which icon each block gets (in the shape it will have), the words for why a click would do nothing, the caption above the hotbar (the piece in hand and its cost, the blaster and what a shot takes, or the reason in red), the card for the interact key (`interactPrompt`: "Strip debris" and how many strips are left) and the key list of Build Mode.
- `research-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the crew's research (5c), as plain data in and out: the pill's points and bar toward the next row of the ladder (`researchForHud`), when the pill is up (`ResearchPill`: always in Build Mode, and for ten seconds after the points change), the padlock on a hotbar slot and the words "Needs research · have of need" for the piece in hand (`slotLocked`, `lockForPiece`, `lockText`), and the messages when points arrive and when they open a material (`ResearchWatcher`, which reads the economy as a screen has it, so a guest sees what the host does).
- `run-results.ts`: the Outpost Overrun card of a lost run: the nights survived, how it ended, six counts, and Retry and New Run for whoever hosts the run (a guest's says who it waits for). It takes the place of the pause card while the run is lost; `hud.ts` frees the mouse a moment after the loss for it and `create-app.ts` gives the HUD the session's `restartRun` as `RunControls`.
- `pause-panel.ts`: the click-to-play card with the controls, the run's clock, the graphics preset and the settings (with `?debug`, a time-of-day preview too).
- `status-panel.ts`: the key hints and the camera and graphics readout while playing; in Build Mode the hints give way to the keys of Build Mode.
- `frame-overlay.ts`: the F3 frame-time overlay.
- `bench-panel.ts`: the benchmark banner and the results card.
- `toast.ts`: a short message that fades (for example, "Graphics: Balanced"). `show` replaces what is up at once; `queue` waits its turn (1.6 s each, at most three waiting), so a night's research and the material it opens are read one after the other.
- `settings-store.ts`, `dom.ts`: reading and saving preferences; a tiny element helper.
- `room-panel.ts`, `room-panel.css`: multiplayer. The Play together card on the start screen, the small roster while playing, and the full-screen notice when a room ends. It draws `Lobby.state` and has no network code; it sits above the HUD layers so clicking it never counts as "click to play".
