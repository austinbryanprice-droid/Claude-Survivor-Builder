# ui

HUD, menus and overlays in HTML and CSS, on top of the canvas. The look follows [art direction](../../docs/art-direction.md).

- `hud.ts`, `hud.css`: builds the HUD and keeps it in step with pointer lock, the benchmark, the camera and the graphics preset.
- `player-hud/`: the in-game HUD of the reference (vitals, compass, objective, hotbar) as a pure view. `placeholder-hud.ts` drives it with the game's real heading, the player's own Health and Energy, the crew's respawn charges and the screen of a player who is down, the player's building hands (the hotbar selection, the crosshair, Build Mode and the caption), the pulse blaster's sight and hit marker, the crew's pool as the counts on the first four hotbar slots (a padlock instead of a count on a material that is not researched yet), the crew's research as a pill under the respawn charges, the card under the crosshair that says what E would strip, the module slot (slot 6, which shows the module in hand) and made-up numbers for what does not exist yet (the torch and the charge after it).
- `vitals-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the player's meters, as plain data in and out: the Health segments and the Energy bar, the tone each turns when it runs low, and the "Charging", "Low energy", "Out of energy" and "Shaken" icons.
- `run-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the run, as plain data in and out: the day and night pill by the compass, the Outpost Core's gauge and the crew's respawn pips under it, the screen of a player who is down (`downForHud`), the objective in the banner, the line on the pause card, the results of a lost run (`resultsForHud`, now with the materials gathered and the research earned) and the message when the light changes, a wave is coming, a player goes down or comes back, the run ends, or the player's own strip of a heap of debris goes into the pool (`harvestMessage`).
- `build-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the building hands, as plain data in and out so Vitest can run it: which icon each block gets (in the shape it will have) and the module slot's (`moduleSlot`), the words for why a click would do nothing, the caption above the hotbar (the piece in hand and its cost, the blaster and what a shot takes, or the reason in red), the cards for the interact key (`interactPrompt`: "Strip debris" and how many strips are left; `upgradePrompt` (5e; a turret's "Upgrade to Auto Turret" from 5f2): "Upgrade to Reinforced Composite" and its price, or in red the reason it would be refused) the tag under the crosshair that says whether the turret, Power Node or Light the cursor is on has power (`powerTag`, 5g2, 5h3a: "Powered" with "Fires 1.5× faster", "Burns bright" or "Passes power on", or "No power" and what would give it), and the key list of Build Mode.
- `research-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the crew's research (5c), as plain data in and out: the pill's points and bar toward the next row of the ladder (`researchForHud`; it turns amber and says "Needs Terminal" while points the crew has earned reach a row no Terminal has read, 5d2), when the pill is up (`ResearchPill`: always in Build Mode, and for ten seconds after the points change), the padlock on a hotbar slot and the words "Needs research · have of need", or "Needs a Research Terminal" when the points are there and only a Terminal is missing, for the piece in hand (`slotLocked`, `lockForPiece`, `lockText`), and the messages when points arrive, when they open a material and when they wait for a Terminal (`ResearchWatcher`, which reads the economy as a screen has it, so a guest sees what the host does).
- `pool-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows the room in the crew's pool (5h1), as plain data in and out: `poolFull(stock, cap)`, true when a material's count on the hotbar is at the most the pool holds (the count turns amber), and the messages when a Storage raises or lowers that most (`PoolWatcher`: "Storage built · the pool holds 500 of each", "A Storage is gone · the pool holds 400 of each"; the first look, a screen that has not heard the host yet and a run that starts over say nothing).
- `terminal-hud.ts`: how the HUD shows a Research Terminal (5d2), as plain data in and out: the card under the crosshair when the cursor is on one (`terminalPrompt`: E, "Research ladder" and the points) and the ladder E opens (`ladderForHud`: the points earned, the bar toward the next row and a row for each material, open, not read yet or so many points to go; `player-hud/research-ladder.ts` draws it).
- `run-results.ts`: the Outpost Overrun card of a lost run: the nights survived, how it ended, up to seven counts (the research earned is left out where a screen has no economy to ask, and the upgrades completed, 5e, follow the blocks placed), and Retry and New Run for whoever hosts the run (a guest's says who it waits for). It takes the place of the pause card while the run is lost; `hud.ts` frees the mouse a moment after the loss for it and `create-app.ts` gives the HUD the session's `restartRun` as `RunControls`.
- `pause-panel.ts`: the click-to-play card with the controls, the run's clock, the graphics preset and the settings (with `?debug`, a time-of-day preview too).
- `status-panel.ts`: the key hints and the camera and graphics readout while playing; in Build Mode the hints give way to the keys of Build Mode.
- `frame-overlay.ts`: the F3 frame-time overlay.
- `bench-panel.ts`: the benchmark banner and the results card.
- `toast.ts`: a short message that fades (for example, "Graphics: Balanced"). `show` replaces what is up at once; `queue` waits its turn (1.6 s each, at most three waiting), so a night's research and the material it opens are read one after the other.
- `settings-store.ts`, `dom.ts`: reading and saving preferences; a tiny element helper.
- `intro-overlay.ts`, `intro-overlay.css`: what the intro film wears over the picture: the letterbox bars, the captions, the title card, the white flashes, the black between cuts, the survivor's eyelids and the prompt to skip. It draws whatever frame (`IntroFrame`, from `shared/intro.ts`) it is given, so the film's script decides what shows and when.
- `title-screen.ts`, `title-screen.css`: the page's front: the name of the game, the film's line under it and one large Play button over the live valley, a hint line for when the browser would not hand over the mouse, and a black veil the picture fades to before the film. It draws the words and says when Play was pressed; `app/title.ts` decides what follows.
- `room-panel.ts`, `room-panel.css`: multiplayer. The Play together card on the start screen, the small roster while playing, and the full-screen notice when a room ends. It draws `Lobby.state` and has no network code; it sits above the HUD layers so clicking it never counts as "click to play".
