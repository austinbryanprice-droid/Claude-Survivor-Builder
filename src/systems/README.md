# systems

Survival, waves, economy and upgrades: the host simulation. No three.js, DOM, engine or net imports, so it runs in a Worker and under Vitest. Phases 4 and 5 fill this in.

- `survival/sim.ts`: `SurvivalSim`, the run as the host plays it. It advances in fixed 20 Hz steps (`SIM_HZ`), fed real time by `advance(dt)` (a gap past `maxCatchUpSeconds` is dropped, not replayed) or jumped by `warp(seconds)` for tests and the debug hook, and it announces each change of light as an event. It is the day and night clock and the Outpost Core's health: the Core mends by day, is wounded by `hurtCore` (the debug hook until enemies exist), and when it falls the run is over, the clock stops and an `overrun` event goes out. It also keeps a pair of meters for every player in the room (`addPlayer`, `removePlayer`, `observe` where they are, `hurtPlayer`, `spendEnergy` for what they build and fire, `readVitals`) and steps them with the clock. The machines and the waves arrive in the next parts of Phase 4.
- `survival/vitals.ts`: `PlayerVitals`, one player's Health and Energy as the host keeps them, with no clock of its own: Energy drains by living, sprinting, wounds and building, is refilled in the Core's charge field and slows the legs and the hands below a quarter of the bar (`speedScaleFor`, `handScaleFor`); Health mends after a quiet spell while there is Energy. What leaves it is whole points.
- `survival/balance.ts`: the table of numbers that shape the loop (the Core's health and how fast it mends by day, and `VITALS`: both meters, what spends and refills Energy, and what low Energy does). The length of the day and the night is `DAY_SECONDS` and `NIGHT_SECONDS` in `src/shared/daycycle.ts`, because the sky reads them too.
