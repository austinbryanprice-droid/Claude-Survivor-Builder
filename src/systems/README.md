# systems

Survival, waves, economy and upgrades: the host simulation. No three.js, DOM, engine or net imports, so it runs in a Worker and under Vitest. Phases 4 and 5 fill this in.

- `survival/sim.ts`: `SurvivalSim`, the run as the host plays it. It advances in fixed 20 Hz steps (`SIM_HZ`), fed real time by `advance(dt)` (a gap past `maxCatchUpSeconds` is dropped, not replayed) or jumped by `warp(seconds)` for tests and the debug hook, and it announces each change of light as an event. It is the day and night clock and the Outpost Core's health: the Core mends by day, is wounded by `hurtCore` (the debug hook until enemies exist), and when it falls the run is over, the clock stops and an `overrun` event goes out. It also keeps a pair of meters for every player in the room (`addPlayer`, `removePlayer`, `observe` where they are, `hurtPlayer`, `spendEnergy` for what they build and fire, `readVitals`) and steps them with the clock. Where the world gives it ground and a Core to walk to (`nav`), it also plays the machines (`enemies`): their bites are wounds to the players and the Core, a block that changes is told to the map (`blockChanged`), and the walls they chew through come out of `drainBreaks` for the host to break. The waves arrive in 4f.
- `survival/vitals.ts`: `PlayerVitals`, one player's Health and Energy as the host keeps them, with no clock of its own: Energy drains by living, sprinting, wounds and building, is refilled in the Core's charge field and slows the legs and the hands below a quarter of the bar (`speedScaleFor`, `handScaleFor`); Health mends after a quiet spell while there is Energy. What leaves it is whole points.
- `survival/nav.ts`: `NavGrid`, the map the machines walk by: the ground round the Core in one-block columns (open, blocked, walled by something the players built, or the Core) and the cost of the cheapest walk from each to the Core, with a wall priced at the time it takes to chew. It is worked out again, at most four times a second, after a block changes, and an enemy asks it which way to go (`aim`), whether a straight walk is clear (`lineClear`) and where it may stand (`pointClear`, `canEnter`).
- `survival/enemies.ts`: `EnemySim`, the machines as the host plays them: spawning on a ring round the Core from the run's seed, then each step one of bite, chase, chew or march, crowding apart, and the walls they chew through. Wounds go out through a `Strikes` interface the survival simulation answers, so it has no idea what a player is. `write` fills the wire's `EnemyState` list.
- `survival/valley-nav.ts`: `valleyNavigation`, the one place that joins a valley (its terrain and its blocks) to the map's `NavWorld`, so the app and the tests build it the same way.
- `survival/balance.ts`: the table of numbers that shape the loop (the Core's health and how fast it mends by day, `VITALS`: both meters, what spends and refills Energy, and what low Energy does, and `ENEMIES`: the field's size and where it spawns, and the crawler's body, speed, bite, reach and appetite for walls). The length of the day and the night is `DAY_SECONDS` and `NIGHT_SECONDS` in `src/shared/daycycle.ts`, because the sky reads them too.
