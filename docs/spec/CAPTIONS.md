# Visual Reference Captions

These images define the target visual quality and style for Ashbound Outpost. The goal is high-fidelity lighting and atmosphere with a refined, less purely cubic building aesthetic.

## 01_golden_hour_village_lighting.jpg
Strong golden-hour lighting, soft volumetric light, reflective water, detailed wooden and stone structures. Target for warm directional lighting and material readability.

## 02_forest_water_reflections.jpg
Directional light, long shadows, water reflections, layered trees. Shows what good lighting + simple materials can achieve.

## 03_dense_forest_atmosphere.jpg
Rich foliage density, soft atmospheric haze, warm rim lighting. Useful for survival environments and distance haze.

## 04_valheim_modular_construction.jpg
Modular pieces with thickness, texture, and structure. Building still feels constructive and grid-friendly, but the result looks like crafted architecture rather than pure cubes. Primary reference for the hybrid modular approach.

## 05_detailed_modular_base.jpg
Sloped roofs, layered materials, and intentional geometry. Shows how a snap-to-grid system can look sophisticated.

## 06_smoother_terrain_marching_cubes.jpg
Organic, less cubic landscapes. Useful reference for natural terrain if smoothing is applied. Player-built structures should remain more geometric.

## 07_enshrouded_style_environment.jpg
Stylized high-quality foliage and terrain. Good target for atmosphere and material quality.

## 08_stylized_high_contrast.jpg
Cleaner, more stylized materials with strong color grading. Alternative if pure realism feels too heavy.

**Key takeaway**: Combine the lighting and atmosphere quality of the shader-pack style images with the modular, less-blocky construction language of the Valheim-style references. Structural voxels must have bevels or refined edges. Functional pieces should feel engineered and modular.
