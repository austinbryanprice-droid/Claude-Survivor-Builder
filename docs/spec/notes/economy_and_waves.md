# Economy and Wave Scaling Examples

These numbers are starting points. The implementing AI should treat them as tunable but should preserve the intended feel: early progress is fast, later progress is earned, and a coordinated group feels pressure by night 5–7.

## Day Length / Night Length
- Day: 8–12 minutes (suggest starting at 10)
- Night / Wave: 4–6 minutes (suggest starting at 5)

## Resource Yield (example)
- Debris node: yields 8–12 Scrap, depletes after 2–4 harvests.
- Higher-tier materials appear less frequently or require research unlocks.

## Example Costs
- Basic Scrap Wall: 8–12 Scrap
- Reinforced Wall upgrade: 10 Scrap + 5 Reinforced Composite
- Basic Turret: 15–20 Scrap + 5 Composite
- Research Terminal: 25 Scrap + 10 Composite
- Unlock Tier 2: ~20–30 Research Points
- Unlock Tier 3: ~50–70 Research Points

## Research Points
- Base award: 8–12 points per night survived
- Small bonus possible for low base damage or efficiency
- Spent at Research Terminal to unlock next tiers and modules

## Wave Scaling (example)
- Night 1: 6 weak drones
- Night 2: 8 weak drones
- Night 3: 10 drones, slightly higher health
- Night 4: 12 drones + 1 armored variant
- Night 5+: Continue increasing count and health; introduce specials (flying or base-targeting) every 4–5 nights

A coordinated group should be able to reach night 7–10 with good building. Uncoordinated groups should struggle earlier.

## Shared vs Personal
- Shared resource pool for all base structures and upgrades.
- Personal hotbar/inventory for tools and temporary gear.
